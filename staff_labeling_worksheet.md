# Staff labeling worksheet

**Status:** All 30 labels and reasons reviewed on October 22, 2026; retained without changes.

Read [the full staff taxonomy](data/staff_taxonomy.md) before starting.
These are the 30 fictional running-forum posts for the agreement exercise.
Use the staff labels below; the Hacker News training labels stay unchanged.

- `analysis`: an argument supported by a specific, checkable fact that carries the argument.
- `hot_take`: a confident claim with no support offered; a decorative statistic is not enough.
- `reaction`: an immediate personal response or feeling about something that just happened.

The full staff rules also explain mixed cases and the final tie-break.
The reviewed labels match `my_staff_labels.csv`, with the original post text
and order preserved. The review was completed before obtaining or opening
the staff answer key. Save the reviewed annotation checkpoint before comparison.
