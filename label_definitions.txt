analysis: A comment that, without primarily seeking an answer or action, explicitly states a substantive claim about a concrete result, behavior, limitation, accessibility problem, or implication and connects it to a specific reported observation, measurement, comparison, explanatory mechanism, or relevant source passage.
request: A comment whose main purpose is to seek information, clarification, validation, advice, or a concrete change, even when it includes detailed background or a hypothetical explanation, excluding rhetorical questions and questions merely emphasizing a conclusion the comment has already demonstrated.
commentary: A comment that neither primarily seeks an answer or action nor explicitly develops a supported substantive claim, instead expressing an unsupported assertion, opinion, or reaction or simply sharing a resource, including quoted wording used only to convey amusement, praise, or dislike.
